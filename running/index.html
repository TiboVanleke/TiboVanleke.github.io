Why this event

The long game, with obstacles.

The steeplechase is difficult in a useful way. You cannot separate speed from technique, or ambition from judgment. Every barrier asks the same question again: can you stay composed while tired?

That rhythm is one reason running fits so naturally beside research. Both reward consistent work, honest feedback, and the ability to adjust a plan without losing sight of the finish.
